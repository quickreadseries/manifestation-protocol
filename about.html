About Small Moments Method

A gentle, practical way to make space for yourself, notice what matters, and bring your intentions into everyday life.

Hello, I’m Ela.

I created Small Moments Method to bring together two things that have mattered throughout my life and work: wellbeing and the quiet, everyday choices through which we grow. I wanted to create a practice that feels human and realistic — something that can fit into an ordinary day, without asking anyone to transform their life overnight or get everything right.

I hold a Master’s degree in Physiotherapy and have spent more than 20 years working in different spa and wellness settings, with a focus on people’s wellbeing. Throughout those years, I have met people at moments when they want to feel better, find balance, regain confidence, or move towards a version of themselves they hope to become. Listening to their needs and ambitions has taught me that the desire for change is deeply human — but knowing where to begin can be the hardest part.

Why I made this method

That is the thinking behind Small Moments Method. Lasting change does not have to begin with a dramatic decision or a perfect plan. It can begin with a small moment of awareness: pausing long enough to hear yourself, choosing what matters, imagining a meaningful direction, and taking one practical step. The method is designed to help turn the wish to grow into a gentle, repeatable daily practice — with room for real life, setbacks, and starting again.

You don’t have to transform your whole life today. Start with one small moment.

A 90-day practice, one day at a time

The programme unfolds across four stages. Each day uses four short steps to help you pause, choose an intention, picture it, and bring it into ordinary life.

01 · RESETPause and make a little space.
02 · CHOOSEChoose the intention that feels meaningful.
03 · SEEImagine the possibility with attention.
04 · LIVETake one small action in everyday life.

The four stages are Identity, Vision, Embodiment and Manifestation. They offer a structure for reflection and practice, not a promise that thinking alone can make events happen. Attention can influence what we notice; our choices and repeated actions help shape what we do next.

Ideas Behind the Method

Small Moments Method grew from an interest in how we see ourselves, how our beliefs shape our everyday choices, and how small actions can help us create meaningful change.

One book that speaks to these questions is Psycho-Cybernetics by Dr. Maxwell Maltz. Maltz explored the relationship between self-image and behaviour, including how beliefs about ourselves can influence what we attempt and what we believe is possible.

This idea resonates with the heart of Small Moments Method: we do not have to accept every belief about ourselves as a permanent truth. We can learn to question old assumptions, imagine new possibilities, and support them through small, intentional actions.

Small Moments Method develops these themes into its own practical 90-day structure: RESET, CHOOSE, SEE and LIVE.

Reading note: Maxwell Maltz, Psycho-Cybernetics. This is a summary of ideas discussed in the book, not a direct quotation. Small Moments Method is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the author or publisher.

Made for real life

You can move at your own pace. A missed day does not erase your effort, and there is no need to perform the practice perfectly. This is a personal reflection tool, not therapy or medical advice. Take what is useful, adapt the words so they feel honest, and let small actions count.
