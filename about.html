About Small Moments Method

A gentle, practical way to make space for yourself, notice what matters, and bring your intentions into everyday life.

Hello, I’m Ela.

I created Small Moments Method because I wanted a practice that felt human and realistic: something that could fit into an ordinary day, without asking anyone to change their whole life overnight or get everything right.

My background is in physiotherapy and wellbeing. Over time, I became increasingly interested in the small ways we relate to ourselves: where our attention goes, the stories we repeat, the choices we make, and the habits that quietly shape our days.

Why I made this method

Big goals can feel inspiring, but they can also feel distant. Small Moments Method brings the focus back to the present: one pause, one clear intention, one imagined possibility, and one small action at a time. The aim is not to force a result. It is to practise noticing what matters and making choices that reflect it.

You don’t have to transform your whole life today. Start with one small moment.

A 90-day practice, one day at a time

The programme unfolds across four stages. Each day uses four short steps to help you pause, choose an intention, picture it, and bring it into ordinary life.

01 · RESETPause and make a little space.
02 · CHOOSEChoose the intention that feels meaningful.
03 · SEEImagine the possibility with attention.
04 · LIVETake one small action in everyday life.

The four stages are Identity, Vision, Embodiment and Manifestation. They offer a structure for reflection and practice, not a promise that thinking alone can make events happen. Attention can influence what we notice; our choices and repeated actions help shape what we do next.

Made for real life

You can move at your own pace. A missed day does not erase your effort, and there is no need to perform the practice perfectly. This is a personal reflection tool, not therapy or medical advice. Take what is useful, adapt the words so they feel honest, and let small actions count.
