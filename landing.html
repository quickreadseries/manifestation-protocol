Does this feel familiar?

You know what matters to you. So why is it so easy to lose sight of it?

Days fill up. The same thoughts return. We react before we have time to choose, and old habits quietly take over.

  • You overthink, but still feel stuck.
  • You repeat reactions you wish you could change.
  • You keep promising yourself you will start tomorrow.

Small Moments Method is a structured way to pause, reflect and practise more intentional choices in everyday life.

A practice, not a quick fix

Four small steps.
A more intentional day.

The 90-day journey

A little deeper, day by day.

The method unfolds across four stages, each inviting you to explore a different part of how you live and choose.

DAYS 1–21

Identity

Notice your self-image, inner voice and familiar beliefs.

DAYS 22–45

Vision

Clarify what matters to you and the direction you want to take.

DAYS 46–70

Perspective

Explore your thoughts, question assumptions and consider other perspectives.

DAYS 71–90

Action

Bring your insights into everyday choices and small, repeatable actions.

A few useful details

Questions, answered.

Who is Small Moments Method for?

It may suit people who want to understand their patterns, reflect more regularly and make everyday choices more intentionally. You do not need to have everything figured out before you begin.

How much time does it take?

The practice is designed around four short daily steps. The time you spend reflecting or writing can vary from day to day.

Do I need to print the workbook?

No. You can use a digital copy if that works better for you, or print it if you prefer writing on paper.

Is it a replacement for therapy?

No. Small Moments Method is a personal reflection practice, not a substitute for mental-health care or professional advice.
